Move to a new phone

Three steps

  1. 1
    Download VCF

    Export contacts as a standard vCard file.

  2. 2
    Open the file on your new phone

    Tap the `.vcf` file in Downloads.

  3. 3
    Confirm import

    Your phone’s Contacts app will offer to add the contacts. Steps vary by device.

The VCF exported by this demo contains sample contacts and contacts imported into this tab only.
